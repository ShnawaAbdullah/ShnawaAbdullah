## Welcome to my GitHub Repositorz!

My name is Abdullah, currently doing my second year of my BSc. in Health Care and Disease Prevention (Public Health Supervisor) @ Semmelweis University ETK.

My main interest is infectious disease epidemiology, specifically hemorrhagic viruses epidemiology, spatial epidemiology, and mapping of disease patterns and analysis. I have ongoing research project on the comparative spatial epidemiology of the twin cities of Komárom and Komárno. I use QGIS and personal effort to create demonstrational maps. 

Other research projects:                                                                                                        -  Ongoing Research project contracted as a service-nature invention with the Semmelweis Center for Science and Innovation Vice-Rector and Business Development on the adoption of the first structured Return-to-Work program for post-stroke patients in Hungary                                                                                                        -  Viral Evolutionary Microbiology                                                                                           -  Genetic Alteration of Viruses

Currently Learning: R Language, ArcGIS, Spanish, and Hungarian.
